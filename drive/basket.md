### RZQ-0018 — /ar/income fails AA contrast on mobile only — month label and the log-income chip

At 390x844 axe reports two serious color-contrast nodes that are clean at 1280x900, which is why the desktop verification that closed RZQ-0013 missed them and why the sweep kept re-filing them under RZQ-0002 (a reference whose title names /ar/dashboard). (a) the month label uses text-rizq-ink-soft/60 on --raised = 3.19:1 — the same opacity idiom RZQ-0013 replaced elsewhere. (b) the log-income chip is text --acc over bg-rizq-green/8, and 8% green is translucent, so its effective background is whatever surface sits behind it: --raised on desktop (passes) and --bg on mobile (4.43:1, fails). The chip pattern is the more important half — its contrast changes with layout, so it can pass review and fail in production.
